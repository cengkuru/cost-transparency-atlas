# Geographic context

World geometry: Natural Earth Admin 0, 1:110m, distributed by world-atlas 2.0.2. Downloaded 5 September 2026 from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2.0.2/countries-110m.json and decoded from TopoJSON delta arcs to GeoJSON (five decimal places). 176 displayed features. Antarctica is omitted from this low-latitude interaction canvas. Dateline-crossing rings are unwrapped to avoid drawing transcontinental lines in Leaflet. Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Package documentation: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas . Boundaries are contextual and imply no institutional position.

Publisher markers are approximate editorial anchors for the named country or subnational area, not geocoded project records, office addresses, national coverage or measured assets. National anchors are deliberately coarse points within the country; subnational anchors identify the named region at coarse precision. Their size does not encode records, quality or impact. Indonesia has two distinct publisher entries; do not merge them.

Leaflet 1.9.4 is vendored from its npm distribution, with BSD-2-Clause licence retained in vendor/LEAFLET-LICENSE. No external map tiles, geocoding calls, access token or paid service is required.
